---
name: in-your-own-words
description: "Restate the user's goals and the problem in your own words when they ask for an understanding check or say in-your-own-words."
---

Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve.

Use the conversation as evidence. Distinguish the desired outcome, the problem it addresses, and important constraints or unresolved choices. Keep it concise and let the user correct the understanding. This is an understanding check, not permission to start implementing, invent requirements, or launch a grilling session.
