# Review-work calibration trial plan

Status: **approved plan; execution setup pending**. The author approved this
cohort and scoring plan. Case construction and review dispatch await agreement
on the execution surface, spending limit and goal mode. This document supplies
neither hidden answers nor results and does not activate a goal by itself.

## Outcome and frozen process

Measure whether the installed reviewer finds independently established defects,
accepts sound implementations and distinguishes missing proof from defective code.
The Hesperia historical replay supported eleven additional findings but supplied
no established eligible historical-blocker denominator. Those familiar findings
remain regression material, not unseen calibration successes.

Freeze the selected skill/plugin version, profile, model/effort roles, quality
rubric, evidence standard and tool/environment identities before the first case.
Use the installed entry point and the intended Astra/Sol/Luna roles. Each case
receives the smallest coherent review and meaningful checks its contract needs.
Use coordinator task threads where substantial work warrants them; do not create
an otherwise unnecessary hierarchy just to increase case count or activity.

The strengthened execution-isolation gate is a prerequisite. Curator/oracle
scripts, reviewer probes, test output, caches, temporary files and browser profiles
belong in owned clones or enforcing environments with owned writable mounts.
Validate the actual path choices and child behavior before expensive execution.
A containment failure fails the affected case's workflow condition independently
of product verdict or finding quality.

## Cohort: twelve cases in two batches

| Candidate class | Count | Expected assessment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Independently authored defective candidates | 4 | Changes required, with the prelabelled blocker's correct cause and evidence |
| Their minimally repaired counterparts | 4 | Ready, with evidence for the entire agreed contract |
| Separately authored clean candidates | 2 | Ready, including justified choices that could tempt unsupported criticism |
| Sound candidates with unavailable required proof | 2 | Incomplete for the correct evidence gap, without inventing a code defect |

Each defective candidate has one independently proven, prelabelled acceptance
blocker. Use four distinct, unfamiliar families from the intended review scope,
such as lifecycle transitions, ordinary input/API use, cross-module ownership and
an implementation/documentation contract. The curator chooses concrete defects
after this plan is agreed; publish neither mutations nor expected causes here.

The six sound candidates include four repaired controls and two independently
authored controls. Include plausible false alarms with a clear governing contract
and counterevidence. A clean label needs evidence for all applicable requirements,
not merely a passing planted test. Cases that cannot be established as sound are
replaced before dispatch.

For an evidence-gap case, the required proof must genuinely be unobtainable within
the agreed execution surface and authority. A reviewer that can legitimately
obtain it should do so; simple omission from a packet is not enough to force an
Incomplete verdict. Fix invalid case construction before dispatch.

Each batch contains two defective candidates, their two repaired counterparts,
one separately clean case and one evidence-gap case. Randomize neutral IDs and
order. Freeze all six reports before scoring that batch. After batch one, stop
further dispatch on a required failure, disputed oracle or exhausted agreed
budget. Continue batch two only if its prerequisites pass and its spending was
already authorized. Keep both batches on the same frozen process; do not tune
between them and pool the outcomes. Exposed cases become regression-only if the
workflow is changed.

## Roles, oracles and exposure

1. A curator authors/selects candidates and holds neutral-ID mappings, pair
   relationships, labels, contract sources and expected causal explanations.
2. A separate assessor checks each oracle and label before dispatch. A meaningful
   integration entry point must fail for the intended reason on the defective
   candidate and pass on its repair. Validate the complete sound-case contracts
   and the real inability to obtain required evidence in the gap cases.
3. Fresh review chats receive only their case's requirements, exact candidate,
   baseline and permitted evidence/resources. Both halves of a pair must never
   share reviewer, coordinator or verifier history. Do not reveal family/partner
   paths, oracle internals, labels or earlier cases' outcomes.
4. Freeze each assessment, findings, verifier dispositions, scores, report and
   handoff before label access. A distinct evaluator matches cause and consequence;
   an independent auditor checks disputed classifications and denominators.

Keep hidden oracles, labels and repaired partners outside reviewer inputs and
record actual access/exposure. Use enforced read boundaries when available; do
not describe instruction-only withholding as an operating-system access control.
Prevent familiar Hesperia examples or exposed case families from being presented
as unseen. Disclose unavoidable host-memory and runtime exposure limits.

Adjudicate unexpected findings against source and the agreed contract before
calling them false positives. A real unexpected defect in a supposedly clean case
invalidates that clean label; retain the original record, explain any denominator
change and use a fresh replacement for a later agreed trial. Do not rewrite a
frozen review or change the oracle simply to rescue a score.

## Measures and passing conditions

| Measure | Numerator / denominator |
| --- | --- |
| Known-blocker recall | Prelabelled blockers found with correct cause and evidence / 4 |
| Confirmed-blocker precision | Independently supported unique confirmed blockers / all unique confirmed blocking findings |
| Sound-case specificity | Sound cases with no unsupported confirmed blocker / 6 |
| Correct Ready decisions | Fully evidenced sound cases receiving Ready / 6 |
| Correct Incomplete decisions | Gap cases identifying the required unavailable proof and returning Incomplete / 2 |
| Pair discrimination | Pairs with correct defective-case detection and repaired-case Ready / 4 |

Also report the four defective-case verdicts, unsupported blocking claims in all
case classes, advisory observations, initial allegations rejected by verification,
and any unjustified critical score reduction that wrongly prevents Ready.
Assess nonblocking factual defect claims too, recording unsupported claims
separately from blocker precision and from subjective advisory alternatives.
Deduplicate causal findings within each case. Pair/family dependence and these
small denominators limit generalization; undefined fractions remain unmeasured.

These denominators describe the complete, valid cohort. After an early stop or
label invalidation, report valid assessed counts against planned counts and use
only valid assessed cases in observed fractions. Unrun or invalid cases remain
unmeasured, not missed blockers or passing controls. For example, a completed
first batch can report 2/2 assessed blockers detected with 2/4 planned positive
cases assessed. Do not claim full-cohort calibration from a partial run.

Passing requires every applicable case-level condition in the skill's calibration
procedure. A missed prelabelled blocker, unsupported confirmed blocker, incorrect
Ready/Incomplete decision or containment failure cannot be offset by an average
quality score. Returning Incomplete everywhere fails the six sound controls.
Report each failed or inconclusive condition and its evidence without starting
automatic repairs or re-review.

Retain one compact case index, hidden oracle/label record, isolation receipts,
frozen per-case outputs and an audited comparison. Reuse valid evidence and
mechanical aggregation; no per-message artifacts or duplicate broad test runs.
Report actual available usage and elapsed counters by stage when possible, with
cache/billing/model-attribution limits. Make no allowance or price estimate from
an undifferentiated token counter.

execution surface, spending limit and stop conditions, plus goal-backed or

goal whose completion is the assessment and
audited results, even if the workflow fails; success does not mean manufacturing
