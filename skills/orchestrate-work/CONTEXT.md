# Orchestrated work

Vocabulary for work coordinated by an orchestrator, divided among coordinators,
and carried out by workers.

## Language

**Author**:
The human who owns the intended outcome and resolves questions that the accepted
requirements and available evidence cannot answer.
_Avoid_: Approver, supervisor.

**Orchestrator**:
The agent accountable for the whole goal, shared decisions, coordinator assignments,
and final acceptance.
_Avoid_: Mastermind, global worker.

**Coordinator**:
The agent accountable for one workstream, its worker assignments, and the review
of its results.
_Avoid_: Manager, intermediary.

**Worker**:
The agent responsible for a bounded assignment and the evidence supporting its result.
_Avoid_: Workhorse, underling.

**Goal**:
The author-requested outcome with defined scope and evidence sufficient to establish
completion.
_Avoid_: Activity, milestone.

**Workstream**:
A coherent part of the goal with one coordinator and identifiable dependencies
on other workstreams.
_Avoid_: Department, arbitrary file group.

**Task contract**:
The versioned definition of an assignment's outcome, constraints, ownership,
inputs, explicitly delegated choices, and acceptance criteria.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, vague brief.

**Artifact**:
A work product identified by a specific revision, such as research, code, a report,
or verification evidence.
_Avoid_: Message, acknowledgment.

**Manifest**:
An index of a reported result's artifacts, their revisions, purposes, evidence,
and review and integration states.
_Avoid_: Transcript, combined output.

**Message**:
A directed communication with an identity, sender, recipients, purpose, and
references to the work or decisions it concerns.
_Avoid_: Artifact, implicit notification.

**Acknowledgment**:
A recipient's statement of the observed handling of a message, such as receipt,
application, or a stopped assignment.
_Avoid_: Acceptance, approval.

**Acceptance**:
A responsible parent's evidence-backed decision that a particular artifact or
assignment satisfies its applicable contract.
_Avoid_: Receipt, completion claim.

**Integration**:
The incorporation of accepted changes into the target artifact or system.
_Avoid_: Awareness, forwarded summary.

**Decision**:
A recorded resolution with an authoritative basis and an identified scope of effect.
_Avoid_: Hypothesis, preference inferred by an agent.

**Dependency hold**:
A temporary stop on work that depends on an unresolved question, invalid input,
or unapplied decision.
_Avoid_: Whole-goal pause, cancellation.

**Goal pause**:
A stop of the goal's execution explicitly requested by the author, including work
that could otherwise continue independently.
_Avoid_: Dependency hold, blocked assignment.

**Established independence**:
Evidence that an authorized assignment's inputs, behavior, and resources cannot
be affected by any unresolved alternative under consideration.
_Avoid_: Missing dependency entry, presumed independence.
